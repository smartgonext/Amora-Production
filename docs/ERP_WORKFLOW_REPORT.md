## System Overview

**SmartGoNext** is a complete business management software (ERP) that handles all operations from customer management to accounting. It has **20 modules** that work together seamlessly.

### How the System Flows:

```
Login → Dashboard → Master Data (Products/Customers/Suppliers) 
→ Transactions (Sales/Purchases/Billing) 
→ Operations (Inventory/Rentals/AMC/HR) 
→ Finance & Reports
```

### Key Highlights:
- ✅ **20+ integrated modules** for complete business management
- ✅ **Multi-branch support** - manage multiple business locations
- ✅ **Multi-user** with role-based access (Admin, Manager, Staff)
- ✅ **GST compliant** - all tax calculations built-in
- ✅ **Secure** - password protected with data isolation
- ✅ **Cloud/Desktop** - accessible from anywhere

## 1. Login & Authentication

**Purpose:** Secure access to the ERP system

**Workflow Path:** `User → Login → Dashboard (based on role)`

**What it does:**
- User logs in with email and password
- System checks user role (Admin, Manager, Staff)
- Each user sees only what they are allowed to see
- Each company has its own separate data (Multi-tenant)
- Session management keeps you logged in securely

## 2. Dashboard

**Purpose:** Central command center - overview of your entire business

**Workflow Path:** `Login → Dashboard → View KPIs → Navigate to any module`

**What you see:**
- **Key Numbers (KPIs):**
  - Total Sales Revenue (₹)
  - Total Registered Customers
  - Total Products & Equipment
  - Active Invoices
- **Charts & Graphs:**
  - Revenue Pipeline - monthly sales trend (Line Chart)
  - Weekly Activity Volume - check-ins & bookings (Bar Chart)
- **Quick Tools:**
  - Pinned Favourites (bookmark frequently used pages)
  - Recently Used Records (last accessed items)
  - Personal Reminders (sticky notes)
  - Quick Calculator
- **Notifications:** System alerts and updates

---

## 3. Products Management

**Purpose:** Manage your product and service catalog

**Workflow Path:** `Dashboard → Products → Add/Edit/View Products → Inventory updates`

**Features:**
- Add new products with:
  - Product Name & SKU (Stock Keeping Unit)
  - HSN Code (for GST)
  - GST Rate (5%, 12%, 18%, 28%)
  - Purchase Price & Selling Price
  - Minimum Stock Level (low stock alerts)
  - Opening Stock Quantity
- Search products by name or SKU
- Edit product details
- Activate/Deactivate products
- Delete products with confirmation
- View product details in popup

---

## 4. Customers

**Purpose:** Complete customer relationship management

**Workflow Path:** `Dashboard → Customers → Add/View/Edit → Link to Sales & Billing`

**Features:**
- Add new customers with:
  - Name, Phone, Email, Address
  - City, State, Pincode
  - GSTIN (GST Number)
  - PAN Number
- Set **Credit Limit** & **Credit Period** (days)
- Search by name, phone, or email
- View complete customer profile
- Edit & update information
- Filter by Active/Inactive status
- Safe delete with confirmation
